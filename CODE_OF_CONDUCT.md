# Code of conduct

## The standard

Be respectful, be direct, and argue about the work rather than about the person. Assume the other
party is competent and acting in good faith until shown otherwise.

Specifically, in issues, pull requests and reviews:

- **Criticise the code, the design or the claim** — never the author.
- **Bring evidence.** "This is wrong" is an opinion; "this fails on empty input, here is the
  case" is a contribution.
- **Accept a correction gracefully**, and offer one the same way.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning language of any kind.

## Scope

This applies in every rak200 repository — issues, pull requests, reviews, commit messages and
discussions — and to anyone taking part, maintainer included.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to **rak.ricardo@windowslive.com**. Reports are handled privately. The
maintainer may edit, hide or delete contributions that violate this standard, and may block an
account from the repositories.
